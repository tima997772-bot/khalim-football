Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

1. Overview

Khalim Football is a football content management and analytics service. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when a user connects a supported social-media account, including TikTok.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the service may process account identifiers, profile information, statistics, public video information, uploaded media, captions, and information required to publish content.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide account connection, content management, analytics, video upload, and authorized publishing features. We do not sell personal information.

4. TikTok authorization

Access to TikTok data is based on the permissions explicitly authorized by the user. The service only requests permissions needed for the enabled features.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Users may request deletion of information associated with their account by contacting the service operator.

7. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of their personal information.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the contact method provided by the service operator.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
