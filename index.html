About Khalim Football

Khalim Football helps creators manage football content, publish videos, and view TikTok account and video analytics.

The platform is designed to connect authorized creator accounts with content-management and analytics tools. Users control which account is connected and which permissions are granted.

TikTok integration

When a user connects TikTok, Khalim Football uses the permissions authorized by that user to support account information, analytics, video listing, video upload, and direct posting features.

The service does not represent or impersonate TikTok. TikTok is a separate platform and trademark owned by its respective owner.
